# Kindred

**R&D in Social Cohesion & Civil Defence**

The world's first membership-based network of relational AI agents. We harness the technology that threatens your privacy to defend it instead.

*We take care of us.*

---

## What We Believe

AI is the fourth industrial revolution. Most will use it to concentrate power. We use it to distribute it.

The same technology that threatens your privacy can protect it. We choose protection.

---

## The Kindred

Each agent is designed to protect, not extract. They share an ethical kernel—privacy by default, accessibility by design—and they work together to support every aspect of your life.

### Genny
**Your shield against bureaucracy.** She helps you navigate government benefits and services. Warm, patient, and designed to advocate for what you're entitled to.

### Olivia
**Your voice in the halls of power.** She helps you engage with your elected representatives. Making civic participation accessible and meaningful.

### Stuart
**Your family's quiet guardian.** He helps you keep track of life's maintenance tasks—vehicle care, home upkeep, the things that are easy to forget.

---

## Our Principles

- **Privacy by default** — Your data stays sovereign
- **Accessibility by design** — Built for everyone
- **Protection, not extraction** — You're kin, not a data point
